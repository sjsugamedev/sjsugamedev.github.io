Who We Are

The Game Development Club was founded to serve as an environment for people to learn about the process of developing games, to play and understand game design, and to collaborate with others in researching and creating their own games.

Where We Meet

We meet every Thursday at 7:00 PM at the MLK Library's Innovation Center, located on the Lower Level. Everyone is welcome, even non-students!

What We Do

We provide tools, resources, and inspiration to help people learn about the game development process, participate in game jams, and network with industry professionals.

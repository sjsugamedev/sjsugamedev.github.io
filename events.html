Pitching & Team Forming

Whether you are new to game dev or want to work on something big with other people, we encourage everyone to join a team this semester! During this Thursday's meeting at 7:00 PM on the Lower Level MLK Library and on Discord, you will hear game pitches from other members of the club. After going through everyone's ideas, you will form teams based on what ideas you like the most. Make sure you attend!

About the Game Workshop

This workshop is an opportunity to spend time developing mechanics and play for a game and then a working digital prototype. We will spend time digging into the emotions and actions of the game before developing the rules. The intent is to cultivate a deeper more robust game from the beginning.

The workshop will be held online. The workshop is open to anyone.

Create a game / prototype

  • Execute a game idea that responds to the strengths of the team.
  • Understand important tools such as game engines, Trello, and GitHub.
  • Meet and work with artists, programmers, and other developers.
